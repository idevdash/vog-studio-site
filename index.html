VOG Studio

A personal tool for writing and posting devotional comments on YouTube sermon videos.

VOG Studio is a small application that runs locally on its author's own computer. It is not a service, not a product, and not available to other users. It exists to help one person — its author — write thoughtful devotional comments on Malayalam-language sermon videos and post them to YouTube from his own account.

What it does

  • Lists videos from a public YouTube channel
  • Downloads the automatic captions YouTube publishes for those videos
  • Translates them from Malayalam to English
  • Writes short devotional comments from the translated text
  • Shows them for review, and posts the approved ones to YouTube

Nothing posts without being read first. Every comment is reviewed and approved by hand before it can be sent. The approval gate is enforced by the software itself.

Why it needs YouTube access

It uses the YouTube Data API to post comments and like videos as the signed-in user. That is the only reason it asks for access, and it acts only on that user's own behalf.
